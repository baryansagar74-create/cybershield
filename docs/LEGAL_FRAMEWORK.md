## 1. Information Technology Act, 2000

### Section 79(3)(b) — Notice and Takedown
Intermediaries lose "safe harbor" liability immunity if they fail to expeditiously remove or disable access to unlawful content upon receiving "actual knowledge" by way of a court order or notification by the appropriate Government or its agency.

### Section 66C — Identity Theft
Punishes fraudulent or dishonest use of another person’s electronic signature, password, or unique identification feature with imprisonment up to 3 years and a fine up to ₹1,00,000.

### Section 66D — Cheating by Personation
Punishes cheating by personation by using any computer resource or communication device with imprisonment up to 3 years and a fine up to ₹1,00,000.

### Section 67C — Preservation and Retention of Information by Intermediaries
Intermediaries are mandated to preserve electronic records and traffic data for 180 days (or longer if requested by lawful authority).

## 2. IT Rules 2021 & 2026 Amendments

The Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, amended in February 2026, set out expedited compliance deadlines:

| Rule Provision | Requirement | Mandatory SLA |
|---|---|---|
| **Rule 3(1)(d)** | Content removal upon reasoned government notice or court order | **3 hours** |
| **Rule 3(2)(a)** | Acknowledgement of grievance complaints | **24 hours** |
| **Rule 3(2)(a)** | Disposal of urgent complaints concerning unlawful impersonation | **36 hours** |
| **Rule 3(2)(b)** | Removal of non-consensual intimate imagery | **2 hours** |
| **Rule 3(2)(a)** | General grievance disposal | **7 days** |

## 3. Central Agency: MHA / I4C Sahyog Portal

The **Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C)** under the Ministry of Home Affairs operates the **Sahyog Portal** (launched October 2024).

- **Operational Role:** A unified, secure government gateway connecting authorized Central/State agencies with designated grievance officers of onboarded intermediaries (Meta, X, Google, Telegram, etc.).
- **Workflow for ITBP:**
  1. ITBP Cyber Cell nominates authorized Nodal Officers.
  2. Nodal Officer signs on to the Sahyog platform.
  3. Structured notices generated by ProfileGuard (Notice Reference, Target URL, Impersonated Officer details, Grounds under Sec 79(3)(b)) are filed via Sahyog.
  4. Platform action is tracked against the statutory 3-hour / 36-hour timer.
  5. If an intermediary fails to respond within SLA, ProfileGuard triggers escalation to CERT-In, I4C Central Cell, or initiates an FIR under IT Act 66D.

## 4. Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023

ProfileGuard enforces data minimization principles:
- **Purpose Limitation:** Evidentiary collection is strictly restricted to accounts flagged for suspected impersonation or cyber security threats against ITBP assets.
- **Access Control:** Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and two-factor authentication (TOTP) restrict evidentiary data access.
- **Data Retention:** Case records and forensic screenshots are subject to automated purge schedules according to `RETENTION_DAYS`.
